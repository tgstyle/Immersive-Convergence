Immersive Engineering
https://github.com/BluSunrize/ImmersiveEngineering

Copyright (c) 2017 BluSunrize

Immersive Convergence carries substantial portions of Immersive Engineering's code,
ported into its own package so that Immersive Engineering is not required at runtime.
The derived work includes the multiblock tile entity bases, the immersive flux energy
storage and its handlers, the multiblock recipe and ingredient layer, the container,
slot and GUI bases, the block, item and block-property bases, the custom state mapper,
and a number of utility classes. Behaviour and on-disk formats were kept identical so
that existing worlds continue to load.
