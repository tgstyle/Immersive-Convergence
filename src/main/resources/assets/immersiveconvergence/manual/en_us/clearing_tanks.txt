Clearing Tanks
Out with the wrong fluid
Fluid input ports accept any fluid piped into them, and a machine holding the wrong fluid will sit idle until that tank is emptied. Sneak and use the Engineer's Hammer on a fluid input port to empty it.
The fluid is discarded rather than returned, so the tank is free for the correct fluid immediately. This works on Immersive Engineering, Immersive Petroleum and Immersive Technology machines with a fluid input; output and storage tanks are never touched.
